# Prior-hackathon research & winning patterns

Compiled from web research (2026-09-06). **No prior Orbio/Pons Build Week exists —
this is the inaugural edition**, so there is no Orbio-specific winner list to copy.
Closest verified analogs + general evidence below. VERIFIED vs INFERRED is labelled.

## Concrete winners found (closest analogs)
- **LatinumAI — 1st, AI track, Solana Breakout Hackathon 2025 (1,412 submissions)** —
  "payment middleware that enables MCP builders to get paid." Closest prior analog to
  Orbio Guard's thesis (payments/infra for MCP/agents). [verified]
- **Whale Tank — best overall, Base track, Coinbase's first AI hackathon (SF, Feb 2025)** —
  VC simulator where users pitch tokens to AI "whales." Event theme was literally
  **"Demos Win Hackathons."** [verified]
- **ETHGlobal Agentic Ethereum (Feb 2025)** — agentic theme; finalists included secure
  crypto-API-key management and one-click agent hosting. In themed/sponsored events,
  agent projects won ~1.19× of prizes. [verified]
- **Solana Agent Hackathon (Colosseum, Feb 2026)** — discretionary judging, no strict
  rubric; winners selected by ecosystem leaders/founders after weekly videos + pitch +
  tech walkthrough. [verified]
- **Tapedrive — Grand Champion, Solana Breakout 2025** — storage infra; prize pool
  funnels into pre-seed accelerators (hackathons-as-dealflow). [verified]
- **Arbitrum Open House NYC Buildathon (Mar 2026)** — 1st Tilt Protocol, 2nd Fangorn,
  3rd EqualFi; judged on **technical execution, product clarity, ecosystem alignment,
  long-term potential**. RH Chain is an Arbitrum L2 and co-sponsors Open House. [verified]

## Why teams win (evidence-based)
1. **Working demo of the sponsor's own rails** with real spend/usage metrics — decks lose.
2. **Infrastructure/payments positioning**, not "another agent app" (agent apps are
   saturated; payments & agentic-commerce are the rising categories).
3. **On-chain-verifiable engagement/traction** (Base's awards were determined by
   on-chain engagement data; "shell" projects were publicly shamed).
4. **Ecosystem fit**: projects that feed the platform's flywheel (here: driving usage of
   Orbio credits/keys and the MCP) get sponsor traction.
5. **Two artifacts judges actually read**: a ≤3-min pitch + a technical walkthrough
   (key rotation, budgets, security) + polished UI.
6. **Public momentum**: post early in builders' channels; visible on X/Telegram.
7. **Clarity of user focus + honest, demoable scope** over broad architecture.

## Pitfalls judges penalize
- Fake/"shell" demos (one-pagers with nothing behind the button).
- All-backend teams that can't demo or pitch.
- Generic cloned agents; saturation.
- Ignoring platform wording/economics (e.g., pitching "token price" for promotional credits).
- Over-scoped builds that aren't finished & public by day 7.
